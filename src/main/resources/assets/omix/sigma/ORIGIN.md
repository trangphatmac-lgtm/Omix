# Jello resources

Copied from the user-provided SigmaRebase source tree:
`src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/com/mentalfrostbyte/gui/resources/`.

These are the original Jello fonts, shadows, icons, watermark and UI sounds.
The asset namespace is changed to `omix:sigma/`; image and font contents are unmodified.
Music playback and its interface are excluded from this port.

Activate, deactivate, click and profile-switch MP3 sounds are converted to OGG/Vorbis
for Minecraft's native sound engine. No music or music service endpoints are included.
